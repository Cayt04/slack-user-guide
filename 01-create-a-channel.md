# How to create a workspace and your first channel in Slack

**Time:** 4 minutes | **You will need:** An email address

A workspace is your team's home in Slack, and channels are the spaces inside it where conversations happen. In this guide, you will create a workspace, then create a channel and post a first message.

## Steps

1. Go to `app.slack.com` and sign in.
2. Select **Create a new workspace**.
   ![The Slack welcome page with the Create a new workspace button](images/channel-01-create-workspace.png)
3. [Name your workspace and click on **Next**.]
4. [Type in your name, then add a photo if you wish. Click on **Next**.]
5. [Invite your teammates. You can skip this step using the **Skip this step** button.]
6. A pop up window appears.
    ![The pop up window](images/channel-02-pop-up-window.png)
8. Answer the questions on how you want to use Slack, and then, **Next**.
9. Your new channel window pops up.
    ![The new channel window](images/channel-03-new-channel.png)
10. At the top of the screen, click on the pencil icon and edit details about your new channel. 
11. Select **Save changes**.
13. In the message box at the bottom, type a welcome message and press **Enter**.
   ![Channel with a first message posted](images/channel-04-first-message.png)
